CSIR Energy Dashboard — Optimization & Insights

Operational analytics for 35+ CSIR labs: contract demand, load factor, power factor, non-investment savings, and ROI pathways.

See Modules

Overview

This dashboard aggregates electrical consumption and billing KPIs across CSIR labs, normalizes data, and surfaces optimization opportunities with interactive drilldowns and narrative summaries. A comparative view shows best-in-class labs and quick wins per site.

Next: Objectives

Objectives

  • Track core KPIs: Contract Demand (CD), Load Factor (LF), Power Factor (PF), kWh, kVAh, and penalties/charges.
  • Identify zero-capex and low-capex savings opportunities with quantified impact.
  • Compare labs on normalized metrics and produce ranked scorecards.
  • Provide clean UX with modals, cards, and guided analyses.
Next: Modules

Modules

Next: Data Model

Data Model

  • Inputs: Monthly bills, AMR logs (kWh/kVAh/MD), PF/LF, penalties, tariff slabs.
  • Processing: Cleaning, normalization, metric derivation (LF, PF bands), anomaly flags.
  • Outputs: Cards, modals, ranked tables, narrative summaries, export.
Data Pipeline

ETL process with quality validation

Next: Optimization Levers

Optimization Levers

Right-size Contract Demand

Detect persistent MD below CD; recommend down-contract; show potential ₹/mo saving and payback if revising.

Power-Factor Correction

Size capacitor bank to reach target PF (e.g., 0.98); estimate avoided penalties and kVAh charges.

Load Shifting (TOU)

Move flexible loads off peak; simulate bill under alternate schedules.

Quick Wins (Zero-Capex)

Set demand alarms, correct billing errors, enforce shutdown schedules; immediate ₹ impact.
